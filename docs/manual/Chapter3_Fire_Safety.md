# CHAPTER 3 — FIRE SAFETY & HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES
## السلامة من الحريق والمواد الخطرة

## 1. Purpose

- **Public health / life-safety impact:** this is the one inspection category where a single missed criterion (a blocked exit, a non-functional extinguisher, an unmanaged flammable-gas store) can produce immediate mass-casualty consequences. Algeria's 2019 legislative overhaul (Loi 19-02) was a direct legislative response to a series of fires.
- **Environmental impact:** secondary — fire and explosion events generate emissions and, where toxic chemical stock is involved, contamination events; cross-reference Chapter 7 and Chapter 2.
- **Administrative importance:** Loi 19-02 created a formal two-tier enforcement structure (national committee + wilaya-level committees) and gave civil-protection officers judicial-police powers specifically for fire-safety violations.
- **Safety implications:** this chapter is definitionally about safety.

**CRITICAL SCOPE FINDING — Loi 19-02 does NOT apply universally to all classified establishments.**

Loi 19-02 art. 1 and arts. 14–19 confirm this law applies specifically to three categories:
1. **ERP (établissements recevant du public)** — any establishment admitting persons freely, for payment, or hosting meetings open to all persons by free or paid invitation.
2. **High-rise buildings** — lowest usable floor above 50m (residential) or above 28m (all other buildings) measured from ground level usable by civil-protection apparatus.
3. **Residential buildings** (بناية مخصصة للسكن).

**A car wash, mechanic's garage, or blacksmith workshop plausibly does NOT meet the ERP definition.** This scope question must be resolved facility-type by facility-type. Where a facility falls outside Loi 19-02's scope, the applicable fire-safety legal basis reverts to Décret 06-198's risk-study provisions (Chapter 6) and the general workplace-safety framework (Loi 88-07/Décret 91-05, Chapter 5).

**Confirmed NOT applicable / mis-cited in current practice:**
- Décret exécutif n° 04-409 (transport of hazardous special waste) — confirmed unrelated to LPG/C installation or SDS/chemical-storage requirements. Any fire-safety criterion citing 04-409 for a substantive technical obligation is mis-citing it.
- Décret exécutif n° 09-410 (security-sensitive equipment) — confirmed unrelated to emergency intervention planning; correct citation is 09-335.

## 3. Scientific Background

Fire safety inspection is organized around three fundamental risk pathways: ignition sources (electrical faults, open flames, friction/heat-generating equipment), fuel load (flammable materials, gases, liquids), and oxygen supply (ventilation conditions). An extinguisher that is present but discharged, expired, or blocked by storage provides zero actual protection — physical presence is not equivalent to functional availability. Emergency exit assessment must verify that exits are (a) physically passable, (b) unobstructed, and (c) identifiable under smoke/low-light conditions — all three conditions together, not any one of them alone.

## 4. Inspection Methodology

**What the inspector should observe:** extinguisher presence, condition, and current inspection tag; emergency exit width, unobstructedness, and signage; fire-detection/alarm equipment (for ERP-scope facilities); LPG/C storage conditions and ventilation where applicable; any visible ignition-source-adjacent fuel accumulation.

**What documents should be requested:** the Loi 19-02 wilaya fire-safety operating-user authorization (for ERP-scope facilities only); the internal intervention plan under Décret 09-335; for LPG/C activities: documentation under Décret 21-430.

**What measurements should be taken:** none in the usual field-instrument sense — this chapter is primarily observational. However, exit width can be physically verified against the 0.90m minimum (see Section 6).

**What evidence should be collected:** photographs of extinguishers (showing inspection tag/date), emergency exits (showing width and signage), and any observed obstruction or hazardous accumulation; copy of the internal intervention plan; copy of the wilaya fire-safety authorization (ERP-scope facilities only).

## 5. Compliance Criteria

- **Compliant:** functional extinguishers (current tag, correct agent for activity-specific fire class), unobstructed exits with functioning signage, internal intervention plan present, wilaya authorization held (ERP-scope facilities).
- **Minor non-compliance:** extinguisher tag expired by <3 months with no evidence of actual discharge; exit sign non-functional but exit physically passable and unobstructed.
- **Major non-compliance:** one extinguisher discharged/expired/wrong agent without replacement; internal intervention plan absent or generic; wilaya authorization absent for an ERP-scope facility (first finding, grace period potentially applicable).
- **Critical non-compliance:** emergency exit physically blocked; no extinguishers at all; unmanaged flammable-gas store (LPG/C without any detection/ventilation provisions); evidence of an imminent fire-initiation condition.
- **Not applicable:** specific LPG/C criteria inapplicable to facilities with no gas infrastructure; Loi 19-02 authorization inapplicable to non-ERP facility types.

## 6. Reference Values

**Emergency exit minimum width [LOI — 1976 implementing regulation, preserved by Loi 19-02 art. 46]:** 0.90m. Confirmed as still applicable.

**Loi 19-02 sanctions ladder [LOI]:**
- Imprisonment 2–5 years and/or fine 200,000–1,000,000 DZD for serious violations
- Aggravated to 5–10 years / 1,000,000–2,000,000 DZD where death or injury results

**Extinguisher inspection interval [PRATIQUE]:** annual inspection by a certified technician is standard practice; specific Algerian legal mandate not independently confirmed at this drafting level.

**LPG/C installation licensing [LOI]:** Décret 21-430 governs conditions for practicing this activity. Specific technical storage conditions, tool requirements, and maintenance-log obligations: **article-level detail not yet retrieved — flagged as open.**

**Open item — Loi 19-02 numeric evacuation/extinguisher standards:** art. 46 preserves 1976-era implementing texts. These older texts are the correct search target for specific numbers (m² per extinguisher, evacuation time); their full content was not extracted at this drafting stage.

## 8. Frequent Non-Conformities

Based on the SafeInspect checklist audit (Session 4): the most common citation error is using Décret 04-409 (hazardous waste transport) and Décret 09-410 (security equipment) as legal bases for fire-safety and emergency-plan criteria respectively. Both are confirmed incorrect. The Loi 19-02 scope question (ERP vs. non-ERP) is the most consequential structural gap: applying Loi 19-02's authorization requirement to facility types that may not legally be ERPs could produce both false non-conformities (for non-ERP facilities) and false compliance (where the wrong legal basis is applied).

## 9. Inspector Guidance

- **Red flag:** an internal intervention plan that is a printed generic template with no facility-specific evacuation routes, assembly points, or responsible-person identities should be treated as absent, not merely deficient.
- **Common misinterpretation:** a facility operator arguing that their Décret 06-198 classified-establishment operating license covers fire safety is incorrect — Loi 19-02 art. 20's wilaya authorization is a separate, non-substitutable instrument (for ERP-scope facilities).
- **Good practice:** physically test emergency exit doors (open them) rather than simply noting their presence — a door that is locked, rusted shut, or blocked on the far side is functionally absent.
- **Situation requiring additional investigation:** any LPG/C installation without documented maintenance under Décret 21-430 requires immediate escalation regardless of the unresolved article-level detail — the existence of the licensing obligation itself is confirmed.

## 10. Decision Guidance

- A physically blocked emergency exit or a completely unmanaged LPG/C store should independently drive a **critical** classification regardless of aggregate score.
- Missing intervention plan (absent, not just outdated) for an ERP-scope facility is **major** — it represents a complete failure of the planning obligation, not a documentary deficiency.
- Extinguisher tag expired by less than 3 months with no evidence of actual discharge is **minor** — an administrative gap, not an active hazard.
