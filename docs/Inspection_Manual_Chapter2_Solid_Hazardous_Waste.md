# CHAPTER 2 — SOLID & HAZARDOUS WASTE
## النفايات الصلبة والخاصة الخطرة

## 1. Purpose

- **Public health impact:** improperly stored or mixed hazardous waste (chemical, biological, sharps) creates direct exposure risk to workers, waste handlers, and — where uncollected — the surrounding public.
- **Environmental impact:** uncontrolled hazardous waste is a primary contamination pathway to soil and groundwater, and improperly incinerated waste is an air-quality pathway (cross-reference Chapter 6).
- **Administrative importance:** Algeria operates a full, coded waste-classification and chain-of-custody regulatory system (Décret 06-104's waste list, the declaration/collection/transport/treatment permit chain) — this is one of the more procedurally complete categories in Algerian environmental law, and inspection should verify the actual chain, not just the presence of bins.
- **Safety implications:** mixed incompatible waste (e.g., oxidizers with combustibles) is a fire/explosion risk; cross-reference Chapter 3 (Fire Safety).

## 3. Scientific Background

Waste is classified in Algerian law (Décret 06-104) by a coded system analogous in structure to internationally-used waste catalogues: each waste type receives a code that determines its handling regime (ordinary/household, inert, special, special hazardous). This classification is the scientific and legal backbone of the entire chapter — every subsequent obligation (declaration, collection accreditation, transport conditions, treatment-facility acceptance) is triggered by which code a given waste stream falls under. An inspection that never asks "what is the waste code for what this facility produces" is inspecting storage and containers without ever verifying the facility has correctly classified what it's storing — this is the structural gap identified in the SafeInspect audit (Session 3): only one facility type (UAB) in the current checklist actually performs this classification step.

## 4. Inspection Methodology

**What the inspector should observe:** waste storage areas (segregation by category, container integrity, labeling), evidence of mixing between ordinary and hazardous streams, evidence of on-site burning (scorch marks, ash accumulation — cross-reference Chapter 3).

**What documents should be requested, in order:**
1. The facility's waste inventory/classification record — does it identify which Décret 06-104 code(s) apply to its actual waste streams?
2. The collection contract with an accredited operator (accreditation under Décret 09-19 — request the operator's accreditation certificate, not just the service contract).
3. The most recent transfer/pickup receipt or manifest — proves the contract is being executed, not merely signed.
4. Where the facility itself transports its own waste rather than using a third-party collector: the transport authorization documents specified in Section 6 below.
5. For healthcare-adjacent facilities (veterinary, abattoir with on-site veterinary practice): verify the three-stream color-coded segregation described in Section 6.

**What measurements should be taken:** none — this chapter is almost entirely documentary/visual; no Décret 06-104-linked quantitative field measurement was identified during this manual's drafting.

**What evidence should be collected:** photographs of segregated storage; copy of waste inventory/classification record; copy of collector's accreditation certificate; copy of most recent transfer manifest.

## 5. Compliance Criteria

- **Compliant:** waste classified by code, segregated accordingly, collected under contract with an accredited operator (accreditation verified, not assumed), transfer manifests current.
- **Minor non-compliance:** classification record incomplete or outdated but physical segregation and collection otherwise adequate.
- **Major non-compliance:** hazardous waste stored without segregation from ordinary waste; collection contract present but no transfer manifest available (cannot confirm execution); collector's accreditation not verifiable.
- **Critical non-compliance:** hazardous waste stored with no evidence of any collection arrangement at all; evidence of on-site burning of waste; evidence of mixing of incompatible hazardous categories creating an immediate fire/toxic-release risk; healthcare-adjacent facility with no color-coded stream segregation for infectious/sharps/body-part waste.
- **Not applicable:** facility generates no waste beyond ordinary municipal-equivalent waste (verify against the technical file — an activity type with no plausible hazardous byproduct, e.g., pure retail with no processing).

## 6. Reference Values

**Waste transport authorization — required documentation [LOI]**
*Source: direct governmental permit-application requirements, confirmed via official source (Ministry of Environment permit request page).*

To transport hazardous special waste under Décret 04-409, the operator must hold/present:
1. A request identifying the applicant's name/trade name and address, and the nature, designation, and **code** of the waste to be collected — the code referencing Décret 06-104 directly.
2. A list of drivers with competency certificates.
3. Commercial register for the transport operator.
4. Valid driving licenses for the drivers.
5. Vehicle insurance contracts.
6. Vehicle registration documents ("gray cards") for vehicles and trailers.
7. Technical inspection certificate for vehicles and trailers.
8. Vehicle circulation permit.
9. Identification of loading/unloading points and principal travel routes.
10. The planned transport route and duration.

**This checklist of required documents is directly usable as the "Required Evidence" set for any inspection criterion verifying whether a facility's waste transport arrangement is properly authorized** — an inspector should be able to request items 1–8 specifically, not just "a permit."

**Healthcare-activity waste — three-stream classification [LOI]**
*Source: Décret exécutif n° 03-478, confirmed directly with stream-level detail.*

| Stream | Waste type | Handling procedure |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Body-part-derived waste | Dedicated procedure sequence; governed additionally by a joint ministerial order (4 avril 2011) on treatment of waste composed of body parts |
| Yellow | Infectious waste | Dedicated procedure sequence, **with a specific packaging method required for sharps/cutting instruments** |
| Red | Toxic waste | Dedicated procedure sequence |

Applicability to SafeInspect facility types: directly relevant to any facility with an on-site veterinary or medical practice component (this may include abattoir/slaughterhouse facilities where veterinary inspection generates sharps/biological waste) — currently **not reflected at all** in the SafeInspect checklist (confirmed gap, audit Session 3).

**Waste treatment facility acceptance conditions — [LOI], now fully resolved.** Décret exécutif n° 04-410's full text (obtained this session) confirms the complete admission chain: a 1-year prior acceptance certificate based on a generator-funded technical file, a daily admission register with 11 required data fields, and arrival-by-arrival verification (movement document, certificate validity, visual inspection, weighing, radioactivity check). **This is directly usable as the evidence standard for any SafeInspect criterion verifying a facility's waste destination is properly authorized** — request the acceptance certificate and, where available, the admission register entry corresponding to the facility's own waste transfer.

**Waste storage duration on-site — [SILENCE].** This manual found no generalized Algerian numeric limit on how long hazardous waste may be stored on a generating facility's premises before mandatory collection. Where a specific accreditation decision (individual to a facility) sets such a limit, that facility-specific limit governs; no general legal number should be presented as if it applied universally.

**Waste export — [LOI, narrow applicability].** Décret exécutif n° 19-10 regulates export of hazardous special waste. Applicable only to facilities engaged in cross-border waste movement — expected to be rare among the municipal-inspection facility types in scope for this platform, noted for completeness rather than as a standard inspection point.

| Collection execution | Most recent transfer/pickup manifest, dated |
| Self-transport authorization | The 10-item document set in Section 6 |
| Healthcare-adjacent waste | Photograph of color-coded segregated containers; sharps packaging verified for the yellow stream |
| Treatment-facility destination | Confirmation the receiving facility holds Décret 04-410 acceptance authorization for that waste category (documentary — request from facility or verify against a public registry if available) |

## 8. Frequent Non-Conformities

Based on the SafeInspect checklist audit (Session 3): the most common structural failure is not missing containers but a missing classification step — facilities are frequently able to show "a bin" and "a contract" without ever having identified which Décret 06-104 code(s) their actual waste streams fall under, meaning segregation, when it occurs, is often accidental rather than systematic. The second most common failure pattern is a collection contract that exists on paper with no transfer manifest evidencing actual pickups — contract existence is not proof of execution.

## 9. Inspector Guidance

- **Red flag:** a facility with no hazardous byproduct whatsoever from its declared activity — verify this claim against the technical file rather than accepting it, since very few classified-establishment activity types genuinely produce zero special waste.
- **Common misinterpretation:** "the collector has a truck with the company's name on it" is not equivalent to "the collector holds a Décret 09-19 accreditation" — request the actual accreditation certificate.
- **Good practice:** cross-check the waste-transport route/duration declared in the transport authorization (Section 6, item 10) against the declared treatment-facility destination — a mismatch is a strong signal the waste may actually be going somewhere other than the authorized destination.
- **Situation requiring additional investigation:** any facility generating healthcare-adjacent waste (veterinary practice, on-site medical station) with no visible color-coded segregation should be treated as a priority follow-up, given the public-health severity of infectious/sharps waste mismanagement.

## 10. Decision Guidance

- Evidence of on-site burning or mixing of incompatible hazardous categories should independently drive a **critical** classification regardless of the facility's aggregate score, given the immediate fire/toxic-release risk — mirrors the critical-override logic already implemented in `scoringUtils.ts`.
- A collection contract with no transfer manifest should be treated as **major**, not merely **minor** — the absence of proof-of-execution is functionally equivalent to not knowing whether the waste is being handled at all.
- Missing classification/inventory alone (with physical segregation and collection otherwise adequate) is appropriately **minor** — it is a documentary gap, not an active harm pathway, provided the physical handling is sound.

## 11. Related Requirements

- **Chapter 1 (Wastewater):** septic-system sludge and oil-separator waste become special/hazardous waste once removed from the water system — governed by this chapter's collection/transport/treatment rules, not Chapter 1's.
- **Chapter 3 (Fire Safety):** incompatible hazardous waste mixing and on-site incineration are simultaneously waste-management and fire-safety findings; the two chapters should cross-reference rather than double-count independently derived findings for the same underlying observation.
- **Chapter 6 (Air Quality):** on-site waste burning is also an uncontrolled-emissions event; cross-reference before treating it as a waste-chapter-only finding.
- **Documentation & Licensing chapter (forthcoming):** the waste-collection contract and transport authorization are themselves licensing instruments for the collector/transporter, not the generating facility — verify which party's documentation is actually being checked.
