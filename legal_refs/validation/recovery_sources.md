
## Official source recovery research — 2026-08-20

The official Algerian Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement site at https://www.joradp.dz/HFR/Index.htm exposes a French Journal Officiel download area (“Journaux”) and a database search area (“Recherche”). This is the preferred external source for recovering missing or unclear repository documents, subject to downloading and verifying the exact official PDF against the Markdown.

The official JORADP PDF `https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2018/F2018035.pdf` is available and opens as Journal Officiel N° 35, corresponding to 13 June 2018. This issue is the official duplicate containing Law 18-09 dated 10 June 2018. It is suitable for direct page-level cross-validation of the local `loi 18-09fr.pdf`, but the PDF viewer’s extracted text is not sufficient by itself; the downloaded/local PDF must remain the comparison source.
